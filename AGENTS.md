# WealthSync Development & Design Rules

All work on this repository must adhere strictly to the following principles:

1. **Aesthetics**:
   - Never use purple / violet / indigo / fuchsia neon gradients.
   - Use deep obsidian (`#09090b`), slate, crisp white typography, and restrained functional accents (emerald, amber, cyan).
   - Never use pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) buttons. Use structured rectangular geometry (`rounded-lg` or `rounded-md`).
   - No cursor animations, 3D tilt effects, or floaty scroll lag.

2. **Content & Copy**:
   - No vague hero texts, buzzwords, or marketing fluff.
   - No fake reviews, fake metrics, fake customer counters, or fake star ratings.
   - Never use emoji icons in UI elements or headers; use crisp SVG icons (`lucide-react`).
   - Never use em dashes (`—`) in copy.
   - Never use AI-slop stock photos or cartoon illustrations.

3. **Pre-Launch Integrity**:
   - Custom domain support documented.
   - Institutional bespoke favicon (no purple gradient, no generic dollar sign).
   - Zero "Made with AI" tags or watermarks anywhere.
   - Full `/privacy` and `/terms` legal pages accessible in navigation and footer.
